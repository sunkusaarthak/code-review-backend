System architecture delineates the conceptual model defining the structure, behaviour, and perspectives of a system. Data flow diagrams (DFDs) visualise the movement of data within a business information system, detailing processes involved in transferring data from inputs to file storage and report 15 generation. They are categorised into logical and physical representations, where logical DFDs outline data flow for business functions and physical DFDs describe its implementation.

Use Case Diagram: Use case diagrams illustrate the interactions and behaviours of a system in relation to its users and external actors. They outline the various actions (use cases) that the system can perform, typically in collaboration with external entities.
